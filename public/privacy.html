Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

LIFORA is a blood donation and blood request platform operated by [your name or organisation], Sylhet, Bangladesh. Contact: [your contact email].

2. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Profile data: role (donor or requester), blood group, age, city, area, phone number, travel distance, last donation date.
  • Request data: patient name, hospital, blood group, units, contact person and phone entered by a requester.
  • Activity data: responses to requests, donation confirmations, in-app notifications, contact messages you send us.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic device information in server logs, used for security and abuse prevention.

Blood group and health-related details are sensitive. We only collect what the service needs and only with your consent given at sign-up.

3. Why we use it

  • To match blood requests with compatible, available donors and let requesters and donors reach each other.
  • To verify your email, keep your account secure and prevent misuse.
  • To send service messages such as verification codes and password reset links.
  • To keep platform statistics (counts only, not personal details).

4. Who can see your information

  • Your phone number and email are not shown publicly. Signed-in requesters can see a donor's contact details only through the contact feature, which is rate limited.
  • Request contact numbers are visible to signed-in members, not to guests.
  • Administrators can see account data needed to run and protect the service.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

5. Advertising and cookies

LIFORA may show ads to signed-out visitors on public pages through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Ads are never shown inside signed-in areas, and we never use your blood group or health information to choose ads. You can manage ad personalisation in your Google account settings or through your browser.

LIFORA itself stores your login session and language/theme preferences in your browser.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app; your personal data is then anonymised. Records other people depend on (such as a completed request) are kept without personal details. Backups may hold older copies for a limited time.

7. Your choices

  • Update your profile at any time.
  • Mark yourself unavailable as a donor at any time.
  • Delete your account from your profile, or email us at the address above.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed, access is role-based, traffic uses HTTPS and login attempts are rate limited. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

9. Children

LIFORA is not for people under 18. Donors must be between 18 and 65 years old.

10. Medical notice

LIFORA is a coordination tool, not a medical service. Blood group matching is only a convenience filter and is not medical clearance. A hospital laboratory and a physician must confirm compatibility and donor eligibility.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
